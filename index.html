Questions

Frequently asked

Can you arrange both flights and cruises?

Yes. Flights, hotel nights, transfers and the cruise can be considered together so timings and comfort work as one journey.

Do you only plan luxury travel?

The focus is business and first class air travel, premium cruises and special journeys where personal planning adds meaningful value.

How quickly will I hear back?

A first response would normally arrive within one business day. Complex multi-stop itineraries may need a little longer to research properly.

Are the prices on this page live fares?

No. They are illustrative concept prices only. A real quote depends on origin, dates, cabin, route and live availability.
