Questions

Frequently asked

Who will personally handle my enquiry?

Patrick Morgan will review your request, research the options and remain your dedicated point of contact. You will not be passed between anonymous agents.

Can you arrange flights, hotels and cruises together?

Yes. Flights, hotel stays, transfers and cruises can be considered together so timing, location and comfort work as one journey.

Do you only plan luxury travel?

The focus is business and first class air travel, exceptional hotels, premium cruises and special journeys where personal planning adds meaningful value.

How quickly will I hear back?

A first response would normally arrive within one business day. Complex multi-stop itineraries may need a little longer to research properly.

Are the prices on this page live rates?

No. They are illustrative concept prices only. A real quote depends on origin, dates, cabin or room type, route and live availability.
